# Customer Churn Note

Acme Support reviewed enterprise customer churn for Q2.

Key observations:

- Logo churn rose from 11% in Q1 to 18% in Q2.
- The highest-risk segment was self-serve teams with more than 80 monthly seats.
- 12 accounts cited slow onboarding as the primary reason for leaving.
- 7 accounts cited missing audit-log export as a blocker for renewal.
- Customers that completed setup within 14 days renewed at a higher rate than customers that took longer.

Current actions:

- Support is drafting a two-week onboarding checklist.
- Product is scoping audit-log export for the admin console.
- Customer success wants a weekly report of accounts with incomplete setup after day 10.

Open risk:

The team does not yet know whether onboarding delay is the cause of churn or a signal that customers were already low-intent.
